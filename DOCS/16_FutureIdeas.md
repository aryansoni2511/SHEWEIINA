# Future Ideas

## Purpose

This document serves as the long-term innovation roadmap for **Shewwina**.

Its purpose is to collect, organize, and prioritize future product ideas, AI innovations, automation concepts, integrations, and industry-specific features.

Not every idea in this document will be developed immediately.

This document helps the team maintain a clear long-term vision while keeping the MVP focused and avoiding feature overload.

---

Act as the CEO of a billion-dollar SaaS startup, a Y Combinator Mentor, Senior Product Manager at Google, Software Architect, AI Product Designer, and Innovation Consultant.
