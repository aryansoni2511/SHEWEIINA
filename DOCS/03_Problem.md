# Problem Statement

## Overview

Every day, millions of people waste valuable hours waiting for services.

Long queues are common in hospitals, clinics, salons, banks, government offices, restaurants, diagnostic centers, and many other businesses.

Most waiting systems are still manual, inefficient, and frustrating.

## Problems Faced by Customers

- Long waiting times
- No idea when their turn will come
- Overcrowded waiting areas
- Poor communication
- Wasted productive time
- Stress and frustration

## Problems Faced by Businesses

- Manual queue management
- Customer complaints
- Missed appointments
- Reduced efficiency
- Poor customer experience
- Difficulty managing peak hours

## Market Opportunity

Every service-based business where customers wait can benefit from a smarter customer flow platform.
This creates a large opportunity to build a unified waiting management solution across multiple industries.
