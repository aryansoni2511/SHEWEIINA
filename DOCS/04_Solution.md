# Solution

## About Shewwina

Shewwina is a Smart Customer Flow Platform that replaces traditional waiting with intelligent digital experiences.

Instead of standing in long queues, customers can join a digital queue remotely, monitor their live position, receive notifications, and arrive only when their turn is approaching.

Businesses gain complete control over customer flow through a centralized dashboard.

## Core Features

### Customer Features

- Digital Queue
- Appointment Booking
- Live Queue Status
- Estimated Waiting Time
- Smart Notifications
- QR Check-in
- Customer Profile
- Queue History

### Business Features

- Queue Dashboard
- Appointment Management
- Staff Management
- Analytics Dashboard
- Customer Management
- Live Queue Monitoring
- AI Insights
